01 — Web app

Offlist

Understand what your energy use really means.

Not another power-price or charging app. Offlist brings your electricity use and your climate footprint together, year after year, so you can see what actually changes.

  • Live electricity prices and CO₂ intensity for 20 European price zones
  • Household and EV consumption side by side, month by month and year over year
  • EV charging via Smartcar, with advice on the greenest and cheapest hours to charge
  • Fleet solution for companies with scope 1, 2 and 3 reports for climate accounting

02 — Chrome extension

Flyver

All your Gmail accounts in one inbox.

A Chrome extension that gathers several Gmail accounts in one popup. Read, search, reply, forward and delete across every account, with the total unread count right on the toolbar.

  • Published on the Chrome Web Store
  • No servers of its own: mail goes straight from Google to your browser
  • Open source under the MIT license

03 — Local web app

Secondhand Search

One search across nine secondhand sites.

A small app that runs on your own computer and searches Vinted, DBA, Sellpy, Grailed and more at the same time, with filters for gender, category, size and price in DKK.

  • Runs locally: every request goes from your own IP, with no central server
  • Translates categories and sizes into each platform's own IDs
  • No account, no database and no tracking
